About Me

I'm a bioinformatics data scientist with a Master's in Computational Biology from Weill Cornell Medicine and about five years of industry experience, including Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. Today I work in drug development.

What I work on

  • Sequencing: bulk and single-cell RNA-seq pipelines
  • Cytometry: flow and mass cytometry processing, clustering and rare-population analysis
  • Assays and imaging: cytokine panels (MSD multiplex), high-content imaging including Cell Painting and brightfield
  • Tools: R/Bioconductor, Python, Quarto reports, Shiny and React apps, Docker and Apptainer, GPUs

How I build

I follow a standard software development lifecycle: requirements, wireframes, design, build, test, release and review. Wireframes come first, in the product and in the presentation. My decks are landscape LaTeX (Beamer). I run my own Linux workstation as a home server, with local AI models and automation for the boring parts.

Outside of work

  • Languages: learning Taishanese, Cantonese and Mandarin to talk with family; I built Polylang for it.
  • Web design: sites for local businesses, from my family's machine shop to restaurants.
  • Linux and automation: scripting, home-server setups and hardware tinkering.
  • Markets and world news: data-driven research on news and stocks.
  • Endurance sports: distance running and distance cycling.
