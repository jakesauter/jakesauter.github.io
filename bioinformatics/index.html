What I analyze

Sequencing

  • Bulk RNA-seq differential expression

  • Gating, or unsupervised clustering

High-content imaging

  • Cell Painting profiling and hit calling
  • Brightfield and fluorescence segmentation
  • CellProfiler pipelines or deep-learning features
